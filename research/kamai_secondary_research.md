# Kamai: Secondary Research on Indian Delivery-Rider Earnings, Costs and Cash Flow

*Compiled 10 Oct 2026. Method: web search and page fetch only. Unless a row is marked otherwise, every figure below was read on the fetched page or PDF.*

**Verification key**
- **V**: read on a fetched primary page or PDF.
- **V2**: read on a fetched secondary article that reports the primary source, but the primary itself was not fetched.
- **S**: snippet-only, or cited second-hand without the original. Unverified.

**Source type key:** IND = independent research or union. CO = company claim. GOV = government.

---

## In short for the PM

1. **The industry already argues about "net per hour", but nobody measures it the same way.**
   - Zomato says partners earn ₹102 per *logged-in* hour gross and assumes about 20% for costs (CO).
   - A union says ₹81/hr net (IND).
   - IDInsight, using a platform-admin-sampled survey (N=2,547), measured costs at about **32% of gross**.
   - A Dvara Research figure, quoted second-hand, puts costs at about 53% (S).
   - Fairwork found that only **2 of 11 platforms** could show workers earn the local minimum wage *after costs*.
   - **This disagreement is the core argument for Kamai.**
2. **Most riders are casual, but most hours come from heavy users.**
   - Zomato's 2025 average partner worked **38 days a year at about 7 hrs/day**. Only 2.3% worked more than 250 days (CO).
   - IDInsight found about 77% work less than full-time.
   - Union surveys, which sample a more committed group, find 55% working 10–12 hrs/day.
3. **Fuel is the dominant variable cost.** In PAIGAM/IFAT's survey of 5,082 delivery workers, 57.75% spend ₹201–400 a day on fuel.
4. **Unpaid waiting is large.**
   - PAIGAM/IFAT: 42.8% are idle about 1 hr/day and 34.1% about 2 hrs/day.
   - IIIT-Hyderabad interviews put waiting at 25–30% of active time.
   - The Economic Survey 2025-26 recommends minimum pay that *includes waiting time*.
5. **Pay rules change often and without notice, and that drives strikes.**
   - Swiggy widened its delivery radius without raising base pay (Mumbai, Oct 2023).
   - Blinkit changed its weekend payout (May 2024).
   - Zepto allegedly cut per-order pay from ₹30 to ₹10–15 (Hyderabad, May 2025).
   - On 25 and 31 Dec 2025, strikes demanded "restore old payout" and "algorithm review".
6. **Policy is moving from paper to practice.** The Code on Social Security came into force on 21 Nov 2025 (PIB). Karnataka notified a 1% welfare fee in Feb 2026. Rajasthan's 2023 Act was still not implemented as of Dec 2025.

## Product implications for the Kamai MVP

1. **Hero metric: "Net ₹ per logged-in hour"**, with gross, costs and idle time broken out. **Strength: Strong.**
   - Zomato, the unions and IDInsight each compute net pay differently (cost share of 20%, 32% or about 53%).
   - Zomato's ₹102/hr already counts login time.
   - The Economic Survey wants pay floors that include waiting time.
   - Kamai can be the neutral calculator.
2. **Low-friction fuel and charging log**, plus monthly fixed-cost amortisation for EMI or rent, phone, data and maintenance. **Strength: Strong.**
   - Fuel is the largest recurring cost (57.75% spend ₹201–400/day).
   - The Nagpur cost stack shows six distinct cost lines.
   - Default values can come from PAIGAM's ranges, then be personalised.
3. **Two personas from day one: casual/part-time and full-time dependent.** **Strength: Strong.**
   - Zomato's average partner works 38 days a year, and about 77% of IDInsight's sample are not full-time.
   - But 55% of union-surveyed workers do 10–12 hr days.
   - Casual riders need a "was today worth it?" view. Full-timers need a weekly P&L and an EMI plan.
4. **Idle and wait-time tracker** (start/stop shift. Mark "waiting at restaurant"). **Strength: Medium-strong.**
   - 42.8% are idle about 1 hr/day and 34.1% about 2 hrs (PAIGAM).
   - Interviews estimate waiting at 25–30% of active time.
   - This also produces evidence riders can use in grievances or policy debates.
5. **Incentive target evaluator** ("Is this target worth chasing? Extra hours × marginal fuel vs bonus"). **Strength: Medium.**
   - Opacity complaints and streak-based targets are well documented qualitatively, and algorithm review was a strike demand.
   - But no source quantifies incentive share, so this needs primary validation.
6. **Pay-rate change detector** (track per-order pay and per-km rate over time. Alert on drops). **Strength: Medium.**
   - Repeated unannounced changes (Swiggy 2023, Blinkit 2024, Zepto 2025) have triggered strikes.
   - Individual riders can't see the trend.
7. **Weekly cash-flow planner** (weekly payout cycle vs daily fuel cash. EMI due dates. Buffer goal). **Strength: Medium.**
   - Half of asset owners borrowed, and about a third missed a repayment.
   - 60% struggle with monthly expenses, and 62% have no emergency fund (S).
   - Payouts are weekly by default.
   - Avoid offering lending in the MVP. Partner later.
8. **Exportable earnings record** (proof of income for loans or rentals. Evidence for deactivation grievances). **Strength: Medium.**
   - 87% report being affected by ID blocking.
   - Telangana's draft Bill and Karnataka's Act create grievance channels and payout-verification rights.
   - Riders need their own records.
9. **EV vs petrol calculator.** **Strength: Weak-medium.**
   - EV riders reportedly earn 20% more (correlational).
   - Per-km cost data comes from industry sources.
   - Rental cost data is weak.
10. **Welfare enrolment nudge** (e-Shram, PM-JAY). **Strength: Weak as a core feature, good as a retention add-on.**
    - Gig registrations are low (more than 5 lakh, against a stated target of over 1 crore).
    - Eligibility thresholds (90/120 days) mean Kamai's own engagement data could tell riders whether they qualify.

## Open questions the evidence does not answer

1. **Share of earnings from incentives vs per-order pay**, and how often targets change.
2. **COD burden.** How much cash riders carry, deposit rules, deductions, and the effect on cash flow.
3. **Real EV rental and battery-swap costs per week** from an independent source (Yulu, Zypp, Battery Smart).
4. **Payout timing across Zomato, Zepto and Blinkit** (weekly vs instant. Fees).
5. **How common multi-apping is** among food and quick-commerce riders specifically. IDInsight's 1.69 counts all income sources, and PAIGAM found 84.5% use only one platform.
6. **Will riders log manually?** Language, literacy and smartphone constraints. Is SMS or screenshot parsing feasible, given there are no platform APIs?
7. **Vehicle depreciation and fines.** Only anecdotal data exists.
8. **Women riders.** Under 1% of the IDInsight sample. Hourly pay about 7% lower.
9. **Fairwork India 2025/26 ratings.** Not found. The 2024 report is the latest verified.
10. **Swiggy DRHP delivery-partner disclosures.** Not verified in this pass.
11. **Dvara Research's 53% cost-share study.** The original was not located. It is needed before citing.
